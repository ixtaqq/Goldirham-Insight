# Asset logos

Local PNG logos for all 26 researched assets, downloaded on 2026-10-01.
The shared `AssetMark` component serves these through Next.js Image.
Logos retain their original colors and aspect ratios. White logo variants use
a dark tile. ETF marks identify the fund issuer: Invesco, VanEck, Vanguard,
and iShares.

Stock and ETF images: [Financial Modeling Prep company logos](https://site.financialmodelingprep.com/developer/docs/company-image-api).
Crypto images: [NVSTly icons](https://github.com/nvstly/icons), pinned to
revision `3ba2624c7776761af53c8b9f0569173e696f7786`.
Exact download URLs are recorded in `sources.json`.

Brand marks remain the property of their respective owners. They identify
the assets discussed by this educational research project and do not imply
endorsement or affiliation.
